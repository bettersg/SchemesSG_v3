# SchemesSG

Directory of Singapore social-service schemes, classified with an NCSS-aligned taxonomy so people can find help that fits their situation.

## Classification

**Need**:
The problem a scheme addresses for the person (e.g. Family, Parenting & Relationships). Answers "why do they need it".
_Avoid_: Scheme type, type

**Intervention**:
What the person actually receives from a scheme (e.g. Counselling, cash). Answers "what do they get".
_Avoid_: Benefit, offering

**Audience**:
Who a scheme is for: age bands and profiles (e.g. Seniors, Caregivers). Answers "who is it for".
_Avoid_: Target group, eligibility

**Category**:
A coarse grouping of Needs shown on the catalog page. Derived from a scheme's Needs, never assigned directly.
_Avoid_: Scheme category, tag

**Person with disability (PWD)**:
A person with a permanent intellectual or physical disability, per the NCSS definition. Autism and dementia count because they are permanent. Mental health conditions such as depression do not.
_Avoid_: Special needs (a different, broader term)

## Submission

**Scheme**:
A Singapore social-service offering listed in the directory, with its Needs, Interventions and Audience.

**Suggestion**:
An unreviewed request from a contributor or the dead-link process, carrying a scheme name and link. Is a new, update or retire Suggestion.
_Avoid_: Contribution, submission, request

**Draft**:
A scheme assembled from a Suggestion by automatic extraction and tagging, awaiting human review.
_Avoid_: Pending scheme, processed scheme

**Published**:
A scheme a reviewer approved, now visible in the directory. Approval makes a Draft Published immediately.
_Avoid_: Live, approved scheme

**Update Suggestion**:
A Suggestion supplying a replacement link for an existing scheme whose link is dead. Revises that scheme in place rather than creating another.
_Avoid_: Edit request

**Dead link**:
A scheme whose link no longer works. The scheme is no longer Published until an Update Suggestion replaces the link or it is retired.

**Retire**:
Removing a scheme from the directory because its link is dead and no replacement was found.
_Avoid_: Delete, deactivate
